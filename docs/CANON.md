# VEILBOUND — Canon Foundation

This file records established world and character canon. Proposed changes should be reviewed against this document before implementation.

## Core Premise

VEILBOUND is an original top-down browser action-adventure RPG set in **Eidol**, a ruined land built over an ancient underground system known as **The Vein**.

Centuries ago, The Vein powered cities, transport systems, environmental machinery, defenses, and other systems modern civilization no longer fully understands. Then a catastrophic event remembered as **The Silence** shut the network down.

Civilization survived among the ruins.

Now The Vein is waking again.

## Kael

Kael is a human masked relic hunter.

Visual anchors:
- weathered pale half-mask
- dark travel cloak
- damaged Shardblade
- ancient mechanical gauntlet fused around one forearm

Kael is not initially a chosen hero in his own understanding. He is a scavenger and explorer accustomed to entering dead ruins for salvage.

## Lyra and Mira — named and gendered, not yet defined

Lyra and Mira stand beside Kael on the title key art as of v0.3.3. Both are women; Kael is a
man, as the rest of this document already had him.

Nothing else about them is canon: no role, no relationship to Kael, no place in the
Greyhaven → Archivist route, no dialogue. That is deliberate. They were named by the owner
without a brief, and inventing a history for them here would quietly commit the story to it.

Until the owner defines them, they exist as figures in the title painting and nothing more,
and no other document, dialogue or system may assume anything further about them.

## The Axiom

The Axiom is the ancient mechanical gauntlet carried by Kael. It has been mostly dormant for years.

In the opening sequence, the Axiom awakens in a forgotten ruin and causes dormant systems across Eidol to begin responding.

Initial known capabilities:

### Resonance
Reveals or activates selected ancient mechanisms and hidden Vein structures. It may also expose certain enemy vulnerabilities.

### Tether
An energy tether used for traversal, object manipulation, puzzle interaction, and combat utility.

Future planned progression concepts include Impulse, Phase, Shatter, and a late-game restoration-oriented ability. These are not yet mechanically locked.

## The Shardblade

Kael's primary weapon is a damaged relic weapon called the **Shardblade**. Its conductor is fractured, leaving part of the blade incomplete and unstable.

The weapon should visibly evolve as relic components are recovered.

## Eidol

Eidol is a melancholy post-collapse world where contemporary settlements coexist with remnants of infrastructure they only partially understand.

The ancient world should feel sophisticated without becoming generic science fiction. Technology is monumental, ceremonial, physical, and integrated into stone, water, metal, and landscape.

## The Vein

The Vein is an ancient underground network spanning Eidol.

Known historic functions include:
- energy distribution
- transport
- water systems
- weather/environmental regulation
- defense systems

Its deeper original purpose remains a central mystery and must be defined in the Story Bible before the corresponding reveal is authored.

## The Silence

The Silence is the name later civilizations gave the event in which Vein systems shut down simultaneously.

The public historical explanation is incomplete.

The true cause is intentionally unrevealed at this foundation stage but must eventually have a specific internal answer.

## Starting Region — Hollow March

The Hollow March is a windswept grassland built over buried ruins.

Visual/environmental anchors:
- pale tall grass
- broken stone roads
- collapsed aqueducts
- ancient machinery emerging from hillsides
- reclaimed relic components used in everyday structures
- huge sealed structures embedded in cliffs

The region begins peaceful and gradually becomes unsettling as Vein activity returns.

## Starting Settlement — Greyhaven

Greyhaven is a small settlement built around the remains of an ancient transit station.

Important locations planned:
- Wayfarer's Rest
- Relic Workshop
- Market Row
- Old Lift Station
- Archivist's House
- Bell Tower

Greyhaven should change over the course of the game: dialogue evolves, new services appear, repairs occur, and side stories progress.

## First Dungeon — The Sunken Archive

The Sunken Archive is an ancient repository beneath the Hollow March, partially flooded after The Silence.

Visual anchors:
- enormous stone halls
- turquoise water
- archive machinery
- rotating cylinders/rings
- hanging roots
- broken bridges
- luminous script beneath water
- monumental doors

Gameplay goals:
- teach basic dungeon language
- introduce room persistence and switches
- introduce water/environment interactions
- teach Resonance in authored situations
- award Tether
- prove ability reuse in traversal, puzzles, and combat

## First Boss — The Archivist

The Archivist is a massive ancient guardian machine protecting the Archive.

Current visual concept:
- stone guardian influence
- spider-like segmented locomotion
- rotating astronomical/mechanical rings
- bright central core

The fight should teach mastery of Tether and use multiple arena states.

Post-fight narrative beat currently established:

The Archivist identifies Kael as a bound user, recognizes the Axiom lineage, and finally says:

**WELCOME BACK.**

Kael has never knowingly been there before.

Why the machine recognizes him is a major story mystery and must have a defined answer before later narrative production reaches the reveal.
